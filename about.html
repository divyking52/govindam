About the house

A family hotel beside the Pink City.

Govindam Royal Stay is a 16-room, 3-star hotel in Jaipur for couples, families and small groups who want a clean base near Govind Dev Ji Temple.

Book directly with us for the best rates and a smooth, hassle-free stay in the Pink City.

House notes

Check-in and identification

Check-in is 12:00 PM. Check-out is 10:00 AM. It is mandatory to present a valid photo ID at check-in. Every person above 18 must carry one. Accepted proofs are a driver’s licence, voter’s card or passport. PAN cards are not accepted. Without a valid ID, a guest will not be allowed to check in.

Children

Children under 5 may stay at no charge when sharing existing bedding with parents or guardians. Extra bedding and cots may be charged, and availability depends on the room. Parents and guardians supervise children in guest rooms and public areas.

Cancellation and no-show

50% of the total booking amount is charged as a non-refundable cancellation fee once the booking is confirmed. If the booking is cancelled more than 7 days before check-in, 50% of the total is charged. If it is cancelled within 7 days of check-in, or in the case of a no-show, 100% of the total is charged.
