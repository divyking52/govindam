Before you book

Children and arrival

Children under 5 are eligible for complimentary accommodation when sharing existing bedding. Extra bedding and cots may be charged and depend on the room. Parents supervise children in the room and in public areas. Check-in is 12:00 PM and check-out is 10:00 AM.

Every guest above 18 must carry a valid photo ID. Accepted proofs are a driver’s licence, voter’s card or passport. PAN cards are not accepted.
