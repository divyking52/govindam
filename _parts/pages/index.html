The stay

A stay worth remembering

Govindam Royal Stay is a modern, family-friendly hotel in Jaipur, ideally located on Amer Road near Govind Dev Ji Temple and the old Pink City. From here, guests can reach Jal Mahal and Man Sagar Lake, Hawa Mahal, City Palace, Jantar Mantar, Nahargarh Fort and Amer Fort.

Air-conditioned rooms come with comfortable beds, clean bathrooms, free Wi-Fi, a television and storage. Family rooms and group-friendly options sit alongside a pure-veg kitchen, buffet breakfast on selected days, a terrace, a 24-hour front desk and free on-site parking.
